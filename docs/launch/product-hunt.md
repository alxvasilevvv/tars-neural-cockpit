## PH-specific copy bits

### "What's the problem you're solving?"

```
Operators who handle sensitive work — fund partners, quant teams,
solo founders — can't ship their prompts and files to third-party
AI clouds. TARS gives them a local-first cockpit with the same
ergonomics as a hosted assistant but with everything on their own
machine, audit-ready, and integratable with their existing
Slack / Gmail / Calendar / GitHub.
```

### "What makes this different?"

```
Three things: (1) genuinely local — model, memory, receipts all on
your Mac. (2) Receipt ledger is hash-chained + Solana-anchored, so
every action has a verifiable trail. (3) Includes a B2B workshop
suite for fund / quant teams to onboard their own agent in 4 phases.
```

### "Who is this for?"

```
- Fund partners drafting LP communications
- Quant teams prototyping strategies they don't want leaked
- Solo founders running outreach without giving Gmail to a SaaS
- Compliance-aware orgs who need an audit trail
- Devs who want a real local agent cockpit, not a hosted UI
```
