# Email — Cold cohort invite to fund partners

> Cold outreach to fund / quant team partners for the workshop cohort.
> Personalize with the placeholders below before sending — generic
> blasts perform 10x worse than personalized 1:1s.
>
> Send via the operator's personal email, not the marketing ESP —
> this is a 1:1 conversation, not a campaign.
>
> Honesty principle: only claim what's in `docs/WHAT_WORKS.md`.
> No "we work with 50 funds", no fake social proof.

---

```
TARS workshop for {FUND_NAME}
```

```
hello@meeet.world
```

no closing line.

## Operator notes

- Send Tuesday-Thursday morning (peak open rate for finance audience).
- Avoid Monday (inbox triage) and Friday afternoon (already gone).
- Personal email > marketing ESP. Use a real signature with phone
  if comfortable.
- Reply within 4 business hours to any "yes interested" — replies
  past 24 hours convert 5x worse.
- Track replies, not opens — finance audience often disables image
  loading so opens are unreliable.
- If no reply in 5 days, send ONE follow-up: "Bumping this in case
  it got buried — happy to send a 90-second video walkthrough if
  easier than a call." Then drop them.

---

## Discovery call structure (30 min)

1. (3 min) Their context: AUM, LP count, current reporting workflow
2. (10 min) Demo the LP-report playbook on their actual data shape
   (use anonymized sample if they don't share real)
3. (5 min) Walk through the audit-grade compliance export
4. (7 min) Pricing + workshop format (4 sessions over 2 weeks)
5. (5 min) Q&A + next step (paid pilot or full workshop)

If they're a fit but not ready, route to the waitlist + ROI
calculator (`/workshop/roi`) so they can self-justify internally.

---

## What NOT to do

- No "10x your fund's productivity" — fund partners hate this language
- No fake stats ("we work with 50 funds")
- No assuming their tech stack — ask, don't assume
- No mentioning $MEEET / on-chain in the cold email — risks
  pattern-matching to crypto noise. If asked in the call, be
  factual: optional, can be ignored, used for receipt anchoring only.
